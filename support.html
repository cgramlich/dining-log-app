Support

MenuCaptain is made by MilSpo Life LLC, Dallas, Texas

Something not working, or something you want to tell us? Write to support@menucaptain.com. A person reads it. You can also send feedback from inside the app, under Settings, which attaches your app version so we can actually chase the problem.

Report something

MenuCaptain lets people send each other places, menus and visits, and share menus with other diners at the same restaurant. If you see something offensive, misleading or spammy, report it:

  • In the app. Every shared item and every person has a ⋮ button. Tap it, choose Report, and tell us what is wrong.
  • By email. Write to support@menucaptain.com and describe what you saw and where.

We look at reports and remove content that does not belong. Community menus are hidden automatically once several people report them.

Block someone

Open the person in Friends, tap the ⋮ button, and choose Block. Blocking is private. They are never told. They are removed from your friends, they cannot send you requests, visits or menus, and anything they share with other diners stays hidden from you. You can undo it any time under Settings, Blocked people.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

  • In the app. Settings, then Delete account, at the bottom. It asks you to confirm, then removes your places, visits, menus, photos, lists and profile.
  • By email. If you cannot get into the app, write to support@menucaptain.com from the address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm when it is done.

Deletion is permanent. Your food log cannot be recovered afterwards, so export anything you want to keep first, from Settings.

Privacy

What we collect and why is set out in the Privacy Policy. The short version: your log is yours, there are no ads, no analytics and no trackers, and nothing is sold. The Terms cover the rest.
