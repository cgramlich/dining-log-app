This week MenuCaptain learned your taste in wine, and how to tip with confidence. Here is what’s new. It’s already in your app. If you see Update available, tap Refresh.

WINE FOR ME

A long wine list can be the hardest page at the table. Now MenuCaptain can read it for you.

Start with your Wine profile. Open You, then Wine profile, and answer a few one-tap questions: what you usually drink, dry or sweet, light or full, oaky or not, what you like to spend on a glass and a bottle, and whether you like to try something new. Skip anything you’re not sure about, and change it any time with Edit.

Then, on a menu at any place with a wine list, tap Wine for me under Help me order. Say what you’re eating if you like, and it picks two or three wines from that very list for your taste, by the glass or the bottle, each with the reason and the list’s own price. It only ever picks wines that are really on the list.

Tap I’m having this and MenuCaptain keeps it for you. Next time you open the app, Home asks how it was: Loved, Again or Skip, with room for a note or a photo. Every wine you rate makes the next picks fit you better.

Enjoyed a bottle somewhere else? Tap the + button and choose Add a wine. Take a photo of the label and the app fills in the wine, producer, vintage, grape and region for you to check. Your wine profile is private, and free for everyone.

A TIP YOU CAN FEEL GOOD ABOUT

How much to tip is a small question that can linger over the end of a lovely meal. In Split a check, tap Recommend a tip and MenuCaptain suggests what to add for okay, good and great service, each with the total it makes.

It weighs the kind of place, where it is and what you had, so a special bottle of wine doesn’t have to carry the full rate. It works from the price before tax, and tells you if a service charge is already on the check. Tap one and it goes straight into Tip. It works with no names added, so it suits a dinner for two just as well as a table of eight.

FEATURED IN PORTLAND

This week we’re spotlighting Portland, Oregon, with three restaurants straight from the Captain’s Table. Each one earned five stars in MenuCaptain from a visit this year. When you’re in town, tap Find the menu for me, then Help me order.

    Momoyama (https://momoyamaportland.com/) · Pearl District, Portland
    A Japanese restaurant serving Portland since 2010, with a long menu of hot dishes and sushi. Try the spicy tuna on crispy rice and the wagyu roll.

    Piattino (https://piattinopdx.com/) · Pearl District, Portland
    A neighborhood Italian restaurant with handmade pasta, wood fired pizzas and small plates to share. Start with the cheese and salumi board, then the tortellini with pesto.

    LeChon (https://www.lechonpdx.com/) · Old Town, Portland
    South American cooking with Argentine and Chilean roots, in a building from 1872. The pan seared scallops, the empanadas, the Peruvian fried chicken bites and the LeChon margarita were all marked Again.
