GROUP ORDERS NOBODY MISSES

A group order moves fast. Someone sends their order, someone changes it, someone passes, and the host places it. Until now you only saw it happen with the order open on your screen. Now everyone in the order with MenuCaptain can hear about every change: the host, the friends invited, and anyone who joined with their account.

A busy order sends one notification that keeps itself up to date, never a pile of them. Tap it and the order opens: the host sees who has ordered, and everyone else sees their own order.

To turn it on, open the order and tap Turn on beside Never miss a change. Ordering with a lively table? The switch there mutes that one order, and leaves your other notifications as they are. Friends who haven’t turned notifications on still get an email for the big moments: the order placed or closed, or the host changing their order.

NOTIFICATIONS FOR EVERYTHING ELSE

Group orders are only the start. In Settings, under Notifications, switch on On this device and MenuCaptain lets you know when a friend shares with you, joins your visit or replies, and when a menu you scanned is ready. Tapping one opens MenuCaptain right there.

On iPhone, notifications work in MenuCaptain opened from your Home Screen. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, and open MenuCaptain from there.

ANSWERS THAT FINISH

Help me order and Explain the menu take a moment to think before they answer. On a long menu, that thinking could crowd out the answer itself, and it stopped in the middle of a sentence. Both now have room for the whole answer. If one ever does run out, it tells you so, and asking again brings the rest.

CAPTAIN’S TIP

    Dining abroad? On a menu priced in euros, pounds, yen or another major currency, tap Show in $ near the top of the menu, under Show English. Each price gets an estimate in dollars just beneath it, and the printed price stays as it is, so it’s still the one you point to. Tap the currency code beside the rate to see another currency. Rates are the European Central Bank’s daily figures, so your card’s rate will differ a little.
