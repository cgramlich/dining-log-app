Dear friends,

Welcome to the Captain’s Table. It’s what we call the small circle of people who use MenuCaptain: diners who would rather remember a great meal than review it.

You’re one of the very first, which makes you our power users. You put the app to the test at real tables, tell us what works and what doesn’t, and together we make it elegant, useful and loved. From time to time we’ll write to you here about what’s new and what we’ve changed because of you.

Here is what’s new. It’s already in your app. If you see Update available, tap Refresh.

Scan a menu, then walk away

The more complicated the menu, the longer it takes to read, and not everyone wants to wait at the table. Now you don’t have to. Once your photos are sent, switch apps or put your phone away. The menu keeps being read, a note at the top of MenuCaptain tells you when it’s ready, and if you haven’t come back two minutes after it finishes, we’ll email you a link.

Scanning is also much quicker. We moved to a newer AI model and tuned how it reads a menu, so a typical menu now takes about 20 seconds, down from about a minute. A menu photographed in several pages is read all at once.

Menus in English

A menu in Spanish, Italian or any other language now offers Show English. The original stays exactly as printed, with the English beneath each dish, so you can still point to the original when you order.

A dish known by its own name, like crema catalana, gets a short line saying what it usually is. You can search in English as well: “octopus” finds the pulpo.

Prices in your currency

Traveling? On a menu priced in euros, pounds, yen or another major currency, tap Show in $. Each price gets a dollar estimate just beneath it, and the menu’s own price stays as printed. Prefer another currency? Tap the currency’s name to switch. Rates come from the European Central Bank and are updated every working day, so your card’s rate may differ slightly.

Collections

Keep a trip together. Tap the + button and choose Start a collection, name it, say “Our honeymoon”, and give it the trip’s dates. Every meal you log between them joins by itself. Or skip the dates and choose the meals yourself, like your favorite brunch spots.

A collection opens on a cover photo, then your favorite places and the dishes you loved, then every meal day by day. Find them all under You, then Collections, and share a whole collection with friends in a tap. A shared collection never includes your notes, who you dined with, or what you spent.

New at the Table

Three places whose menus are now at the Captain’s Table. When you’re there, tap Already at the restaurant and the menu is waiting, ready for Help me order.

See what a dish looks like

When you ask MenuCaptain to explain particular dishes, each one now offers a photo: yours, if you’ve photographed it there, the restaurant’s own, when they publish one, or similar dishes from the web, clearly labeled as not this exact dish.

Sharing with friends

When a friend sends you a place, a menu or a visit, you can answer with a tap or a few words, and they can write back. If they’ve sent a menu, tick the dishes you’d choose and send your picks back without leaving the app.

Sending a place now carries your rating, the dishes you loved and photos from your visits, and a single tick leaves the photos out. A shared visit includes what makes the place special. Select all reaches every friend at once, and photos you share stay visible for as long as you keep them.

The Sharing screen leads with what friends have sent you, marks each one Seen, and clears it away once you reply or save it. A menu you’ve shared with other diners can be taken back at any time with Stop sharing, and if someone shares a newer menu in place of yours, we’ll let you know by email.

Logging a visit

Dish search now looks through every menu at a place, reads descriptions as well as names, and ignores accents, so “tiramisu” finds Tiramisù. Type a section’s name, like “flautas”, and every dish under that heading comes up too.

Had a special? Add it, then tap Mark as special, and it reads as a special in gold wherever it appears, including on a visit you share. Each visit now holds up to fifteen photos, and correcting a dish’s name once corrects it on your past visits there as well.

Around the app

  • Recently added on Home says what happened last at each place: a visit you logged, the friend who sent it, or that you added it yourself.
  • A dietary filter such as Gluten-free gathers matching dishes from every menu at a place, each labeled with its menu.
  • Bar is now a quick name when you add a menu, beside Drinks and Cocktails.
  • Calorie estimates are now a choice, in Settings, so the visit form stays short unless you want them.
  • Our welcome page shows how to put MenuCaptain on your Home Screen, on iPhone or Android, so it opens like any other app.

Chat with us

A chat bubble now sits at the top right of Home, Places, History and You. Tap it to write to us, and we answer right there in the app. A small dot on the bubble means a reply is waiting.

Your meals, safely kept

Every night, your meals, notes and photos are also copied to a second, separate company’s storage, so they stay safe even if our main storage ever had a bad day.

Add the founder as a friend

Chris built MenuCaptain and would love to see where you’re eating. Tap here to add him as a friend, or in the app open You, then Friends, and add @cjgramlich.

Your ideas shape MenuCaptain

Spotted something that isn’t quite right, or wish MenuCaptain did something it doesn’t yet? We read every note. Tap the chat bubble to write to us, or open Settings and tap Share feedback to send a bug, a feature request or a suggestion. Or simply reply to this email.

Bon appétit,

Also fixed

  • After scanning a check in Split the check, a tip you add now goes on top of the printed total, so everyone’s share includes it.
  • The split summary opens at the top, with everyone’s share in view.
  • Each dish on a visit sits on its own card, so it’s clear which notes belong to which dish.
  • Refresh has moved off the menu rows, where it was easy to tap by accident, and asks before replacing a menu.
  • Cancel on Edit place takes you back to the place, not to Home.
  • Menu section headings printed in quote marks now show without them.
  • In their words, a restaurant’s own story, is now laid out in clear paragraphs instead of one long block.
  • Places outside the US now get their town. The app only understood American addresses, so a place in Spain or Italy could be saved without its town and slip out of your town filters. Those fill themselves in.
  • Place search ignores accents, so “cafe” finds Café.
  • Suggestions for dishes, and for the people you dined with, no longer cover the page as you type.
  • The Back button no longer hides behind the clock on things friends send you.
  • Votes in Where shall we eat? are saved as they should be.
  • Added MenuCaptain to your Home Screen from our welcome page? That icon now opens the app, not the welcome page.
  • Creating an account is clearer: Sign in and Create account sit side by side, and nothing suggests waiting for an email.
