This week we head to New York, to two Midtown tables that earned near perfect marks in MenuCaptain after a visit this year. Both menus are already waiting in the app. And a tip for the next time you order for a crowd.

FEATURED IN NEW YORK

Heading to the city this season? Here are two restaurants whose menus were shared by our community. When you’re there, tap Already at the restaurant and the menu is waiting, ready for Help me order.

    Ai Fiori (https://www.aifiorinyc.com/) · Fifth Avenue, Midtown
    Its name means “among the flowers”. Up on the second level of The Langham, the kitchen cooks the Riviera, where Italy meets France, and the pasta is what it’s known for. Try the spaghetti with blue crab, lemon, Calabrian chili and bottarga.

    Osteria La Baia (https://www.labaianyc.com/) · West 52nd Street, Midtown
    A Manhattan hideaway for coastal Italian cooking, with white tablecloths and pasta made in house. Start with the octopus carpaccio, then the stuffed gnocchi with short rib ragù and black truffle, or a whole roasted branzino.

CAPTAIN’S TIP

    Collecting everyone’s order? Open the menu, tap Help me order, then Start a group order at the foot. Send the link to your group. Each person opens it on their own phone, with no app or account needed, taps what they want, adds a note like “no onions” and taps Send my order. You watch the whole order come together in one list.
